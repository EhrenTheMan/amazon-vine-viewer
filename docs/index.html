Made for the hunt

Keep the good stuff in view.

Simple controls for the parts of Vine browsing that take the most time.

01

Scan beyond page one

Choose how many Additional Items pages to scan, then browse the combined results at your own page size.

02

Put ETV in perspective

Sort by estimated taxable value and keep known $0 ETV items at the top—or hide them if you prefer.

03

Skip what isn't for you

Hide titles containing your own keywords. Your filters stay local and don't change anything on Amazon.

04

Spot new arrivals

Keep the viewer open to highlight items found after the first scan. During larger drops, the refresh wait adjusts automatically.

A few things to know

Helpful context, not another feed to manage.

The viewer checks your signed-in Amazon session on a schedule you choose. It polls for changes, so it can miss short-lived listings.

Read how it works ↗

ETV isn't retail price

ETV can have tax consequences and may vary by option. Parent listings show one sampled variant.

You're in control of requests

Open Amazon's request prompt from the viewer, or opt in to an experimental request flow that hands off to Amazon checkout.

Shared estimates are optional

The Cloudflare cache is off by default. Shared estimates are unverified and can be incorrect.
